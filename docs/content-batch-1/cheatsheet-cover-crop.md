# Cover-Crop Selection Cheat Sheet

*Which species for which problem, on which soil. Directional guidance for temperate Europe — shift windows ~2 weeks for cooler/warmer regions. A starting point, not a substitute for a soil test.*

## 1. Start with your problem

| Your problem | Best-fit species | Also helps |
|---|---|---|
| Soil compaction | Forage/tillage radish | Mustard |
| Erosion / runoff | Cereal rye, oats | Phacelia (fast cover) |
| Low organic matter / fertility | Crimson/red clover, vetch | Buckwheat |
| Weed pressure | Cereal rye, mustard | Oats, phacelia, buckwheat |
| Poor drainage / waterlogging | Forage/tillage radish | — |
| Drought / poor water-holding | Cereal rye | — |
| High fertiliser / nitrogen cost | Clover, vetch (fix N); rye, radish (catch leftover N) | — |
| Pests & disease | Mustard (biofumigant) | Phacelia (beneficials) |

## 2. Species quick facts

*Rates are for a solo stand — use roughly ⅓–½ in a mix.*

| Species | Type | Rate (kg/ha) | Sow window | Termination |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cereal rye | Grass | 90–110 | late Aug – early Oct | roll/crimp, graze, or spray at flowering (late Apr–May) |
| Oats | Grass | 80–100 | Aug – mid Sept | usually winterkills; else mow/incorporate in spring |
| Crimson / red clover | Legume | 15–20 | Aug – early Sept | mow/incorporate in spring after flowering |
| Vetch | Legume | 25–40 | Aug – mid Sept | at early flowering for peak N; tough to kill — plan it |
| Forage / tillage radish | Brassica | 6–10 (2–4 in mix) | Aug – early Sept | winterkills, leaving root channels |
| Mustard | Brassica | 8–12 | Aug – Sept | incorporate at green-bud/flowering, before seed set |
| Phacelia | Broadleaf | 8–12 | Apr – early Sept | winterkills in hard frost; else mow before seed set |
| Buckwheat | Broadleaf | 50–70 | May – Aug (frost-free) | mow before seed set (~5–6 wks); first frost kills it |

## 3. Match to your soil

| Soil | What matters most | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Sandy / light | N and moisture drain fast — catch crops (rye) + build organic matter | — |
| Loam / medium | Forgiving — sequencing and funding matter more than soil limits | — |
| Clay / heavy | Compaction and drainage limit first — deep rooters (radish), phased reduced-till | — |
| Silt | Caps and erodes — keep it covered year-round | — |
| Chalky / calcareous | Dries out, holds little OM — build it with covers + compost | — |
| Peaty / organic | Fragile carbon store — minimise disturbance, protect what's there | Oats, deep-rooting radish |

## 4. The 3-way mix rule

One species from each job, at ⅓–½ solo rates:

1. **Deep rooter** (radish) — breaks compaction, opens drainage
2. **Nitrogen fixer** (clover or vetch) — earns the money back
3. **Biomass builder** (rye or oats) — holds soil, smothers weeds

A mix spanning families gives several benefits at once and usually the widest funding eligibility. A single-species stand is fine to start — add diversity once establishment feels routine.

## 5. Budget line

Plan on roughly **€55/ha per year** for seed plus one establishment pass — recurring, every year the cover stays in. Eco-scheme payments where available commonly run €50–130/ha, but rates vary widely by country — check your national scheme, don't assume.
