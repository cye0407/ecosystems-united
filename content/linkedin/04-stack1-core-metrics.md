# Post 4: Stack 1 — Core Metrics

"If it can't be measured, it can't be defended."

I start every conversation here because most businesses — especially smaller operations — can't answer basic questions about themselves:

How much energy do you actually use?
Where does your waste go?
What are your real input costs per unit?

When a buyer asks for Scope 3 data, they scramble. When a bank wants metrics for a sustainability-linked loan, they guess.

The frustrating part? A lot of these operations are already doing great work. They just can't prove it.

Stack 1 is about building that proof. Three lenses:

Your operation — map what comes in (energy, water, materials), what goes out (products, waste, emissions), and the flows between.

Your environment — where do you sit relative to benchmarks, regulations, buyer expectations?

Your budget — what does measuring actually cost vs. what does it reveal?

You've nailed Stack 1 when you can pull your data within 24 hours, calculate cost-per-unit from actuals not estimates, and answer a buyer questionnaire without scrambling.

Not glamorous. Nobody's going to congratulate you for it. But without it, everything else — the efficiency work, the circularity, the certifications — is guesswork.

And guesswork doesn't survive buyer scrutiny.

What's the one metric you wish you had better visibility into?
