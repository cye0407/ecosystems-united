# Post 6: Stack 3 — Circular Value Creation

After you can see your operation (Stack 1) and you've tightened it up (Stack 2), there's still value walking out the door.

By-products you're paying to dispose of that could be inputs for someone else. Equipment sitting idle most of the week. Heat leaving your process that could be warming your building.

Stack 3 is about capturing that lost margin. And this is where it gets interesting for agriculture especially.

Four streams to look at:

Physical — waste, by-products, scrap, packaging
Energy — heat recovery, cooling, power recapture
Assets — equipment, space, vehicles sitting underused
Information — data and relationships you aren't monetizing

For each one: what is it, how much, who could use it, and what's it worth?

The mindset shift is from disposal (a cost you manage) to symbiosis (a revenue line you build).

This isn't theoretical. It's asking a simple question: who nearby needs what I'm throwing away?

I see this a lot with smaller agricultural operations — they're sitting on circular value and don't realize it because nobody framed it that way. It was always just "waste management."

You know it's working when you've got at least one circular initiative generating value, disposal costs are down, and you can point to it on a tender or certification application.

This is where sustainability starts literally paying for itself.
