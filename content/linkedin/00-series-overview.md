## Rules
- Put all links in the FIRST COMMENT, never in the post body (LinkedIn buries posts with outbound links)
- Engage with every comment in the first 2 hours — this is when the algorithm decides reach
- First 5-10 posts will have low engagement. That's normal. Stay consistent.
